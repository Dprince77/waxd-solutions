Our story

Accessible, secure digital transactions for everyone

Waxd Mobile Nigeria Limited is a technology company focused on providing accessible and secure digital transaction solutions.

The Waxd Raven is a hardware and cloud-based digital transaction solution designed to facilitate secure and accessible digital transactions. The system uses dual biometric authentication, incorporating both fingerprint and facial recognition.

Waxd's solutions are designed to support users and businesses in environments where access to reliable data connectivity and digital infrastructure may be limited.

The Waxd cloud platform

Another level of technology behind every Raven device

The Waxd cloud platform extends Raven, matching identities, monitoring devices and turning every transaction into reliable operational data.

  • ◈

    Dual Biometric Matching

    Fingerprint and facial matching against a central user database in near real time, no card or PIN needed.

  • ⬡

    Transaction Engine

    Processes, records and reconciles every transaction across the platform with full audit trails.

  • ◎

    Participants

    Identifies device users and the information tied to their transactions across every touchpoint.

  • ▣

    Device Management

    Monitors and remotely updates Raven devices wherever they are deployed in the field.

  • ◇

    Offline-Ready Connectivity

    Keeps devices operational even when data connectivity is limited, syncing once a connection is available.

  • ✦

    Reporting & Insight

    Turns transaction data into operational insight across your organisation's touchpoints.
