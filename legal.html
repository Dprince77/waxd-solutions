Privacy & data

Waxd handles biometric and transaction data as part of delivering the Raven platform. We only collect what's needed to authenticate users and process transactions. We work within Nigerian data protection requirements, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

  • Biometric data, fingerprint and facial recognition, is used solely for identity verification.
  • Transaction data supports fraud prevention and reporting.
  • You can request more detail on how your data is handled by contacting us.
